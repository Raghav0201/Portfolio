01
Frappe app

Project Feasibility AI Analyzer

A custom Frappe and ERPNext app that helps management decide whether a new project is financially worth taking. It reads the company’s real accounting data, including the General Ledger, invoices, payments, expense claims and salary slips, and allows manual adjustments.

Using the last three months of performance, it estimates costs, profit or loss, profit margin and remaining bank balance, then compares the margin against a 30% minimum. The result is a clear “Worth Taking” or “Not Worth Taking” recommendation with reasons and practical suggestions, such as raising the quotation or requesting an advance payment.

AI Cost Optimiser for ERPNext

ERP systems are good at storing data, but they don't tell you what to do next. We added machine learning to ERPNext so it can predict raw-material prices, spot unusual price jumps and suggest whether to buy now or wait.

  • Price predictions with Linear Regression, around 87–95% accurate
  • Isolation Forest to flag abnormal price changes before a risky purchase
  • Supplier ranking by price, quality and delivery
  • A consumer portal with price alerts and “Buy now / Wait” suggestions
